QuitDays — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "QuitDays" (the "App") handles information. No account or sign-in is required to use the App.

1. Information you enter in the App

The habits you track — names, quit dates and times, daily cost and amount, your reason for quitting, and your restart history — are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data is never sent to or collected by the developer's servers, and it is not synced to any cloud service.

2. Widgets

To show your streak in Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, the App writes a small summary (habit name, quit date, daily cost, color) to an App Group container on your device that only the App and its widget can read. It never leaves your device.

3. Advertising (third-party data collection)

The App shows a banner ad from Google AdMob at the bottom of its screens to support free use. For ad delivery and measurement, AdMob may automatically collect and transmit:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, IDFA)
  • App usage and ad interaction information
  • Crash and performance diagnostics
  • Approximate location (based on IP address)

The habits you track are never shared with AdMob or any other third party.

AdMob App ID: ca-app-pub-4650964756255716~4106420404

Handled per Google's policies: Google Privacy Policy / How Google uses data in ads

4. Tracking (App Tracking Transparency)

On launch, the App shows the system tracking-permission prompt. If you decline, no IDFA-based tracking occurs and only non-personalized ads are shown. All features work the same either way.

5. In-App Purchase

"QuitDays Pro" is an auto-renewable monthly subscription that removes all ads and lets you track unlimited habits. Payment is charged to your Apple Account, and the subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel it anytime in your Apple Account settings. Purchases, billing, and restoring are handled by Apple through the App Store using StoreKit. The developer never receives or stores your payment details. Terms of Use: Apple’s standard EULA (https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the App schedules local notifications on your device for upcoming milestones. They are generated entirely on-device and are not sent through any server. You can turn them off anytime in iOS Settings.

7. Opting out of ads / tracking

You can change ad and tracking settings anytime in iOS "Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking" and "Apple Advertising".

8. Third-party libraries / SDKs

The only third-party SDK included is Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). No analytics, crash reporting, or A/B-testing tools are used.

9. Data deletion

You can delete any habit at any time from within the App. Deleting the App removes all of its data from your device.

10. Not medical advice

The App is a personal tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you need help quitting, please talk to a doctor or a qualified professional.

11. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
